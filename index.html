A streamlined healthcare scheduling platform designed to eliminate patient queues, optimize doctor calendar availability, and maintain structured medical consult records.

Society Complaint Management System

A centralized ticketing and grievance resolution platform for residential societies, empowering residents to report issues and track committee resolution workflows in real time.

The Problem: Lost maintenance requests, lack of status transparency between residents and management committee, and zero historical audit logs.
The Solution: Digital ticket lifecycle system with categorized complaint queues, priority escalation, and administrative action timestamps.

Core Engineered Modules:

  • Complaint Categorization & Urgency: Electrical, plumbing, security, and general maintenance tickets.
  • Lifecycle State Machine: Tracks stages: Submitted → Under Review → In Progress → Resolved.
  • Admin Dashboard & Audit Log: Role-based access for society secretary with date-stamped action histories.
PHP MySQL JavaScript HTML5 / CSS3
